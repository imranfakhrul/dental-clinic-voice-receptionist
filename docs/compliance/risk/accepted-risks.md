# Accepted risks

> v1, 2026-11-26. **No risk is accepted in v1.** Every High risk in [risk-register.md](risk-register.md) has planned controls that bring its residual to Medium or lower.

Rules (DECISIONS #57):
- A High risk may be accepted only with an entry here: the risk ID, the reason, an expiry or review date, and the signature of the sponsor. Whether the advisor also signs is still open (P0-11 open question).
- A Critical risk can't be accepted before real PHI.
- An accepted risk keeps its row in the register, with the decision `accept` and a link to its entry here.
- The register checker (`scripts/compliance/check_risk_register.py`, rule K4) treats a risk ID listed here as accepted.
