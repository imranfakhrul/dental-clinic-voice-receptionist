This Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is made between **[Clinic legal name]** ("Covered Entity") and **[Company legal name]** ("Business Associate"), effective **[date]**. It applies to the services described in **[service / pilot agreement name and date]** ("Service Agreement").

## 1. Background
1.1 Business Associate provides Covered Entity with an AI receptionist that patients use on Covered Entity's website by voice or text, to book, reschedule or cancel appointments in Covered Entity's practice-management system, send appointment messages, and create callback requests for Covered Entity's staff (the "Services").

1.2 In providing the Services, Business Associate creates, receives, maintains or transmits protected health information on behalf of Covered Entity. The parties enter into this Agreement to meet 45 CFR 164.502(e), 164.504(e), 164.308(b) and 164.314(a).

## 2. Definitions
2.1 **Catch-all.** These terms have the same meaning as in the HIPAA Rules: Breach, Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Health Care Operations, Individual, Minimum Necessary, Notice of Privacy Practices, Protected Health Information ("PHI"), Required by Law, Secretary, Security Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured Protected Health Information, and Use.

2.2 **HIPAA Rules** means the Privacy, Security, Breach Notification and Enforcement Rules at 45 CFR Part 160 and Part 164.

2.3 **Business Day** means Monday to Friday, excluding US federal holidays, in Covered Entity's time zone.

2.4 **Unsuccessful Security Incident** means an attempted Security Incident that does not result in unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification or destruction of PHI or interference with system operations, such as pings, port scans, blocked log-in attempts and denial-of-service attempts that don't cause an outage.

## 3. Obligations of Business Associate
Business Associate agrees to:

3.1 **Limits on use and disclosure.** Not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted or required by this Agreement or as Required by Law.

3.2 **Safeguards.** Use appropriate safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 for electronic PHI, to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as provided for by this Agreement.

3.3 **Reporting.**
- 3.3.1 Report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this Agreement, and any Security Incident, of which it becomes aware.
- 3.3.2 **Breach notice.** Notify Covered Entity of a Breach of Unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay and **no later than five (5) Business Days** after discovery (discovery as defined in 45 CFR 164.410(a)(2)). The notice gives the information known at that time, and Business Associate sends updates as its investigation continues.
- 3.3.3 **Content.** To the extent possible, the notice identifies each Individual affected and gives the other information Covered Entity needs for its notices under 45 CFR 164.404(c), at the time of notice or promptly after it.
- 3.3.4 **Unsuccessful Security Incidents.** This section is notice that Unsuccessful Security Incidents occur routinely. They need no further report, except an aggregate summary on Covered Entity's written request.
- 3.3.5 **Who notifies individuals.** Covered Entity notifies Individuals, the Secretary and, where required, the media, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. `[Counsel: confirm]`

3.4 **Subcontractors.**
- 3.4.1 In accordance with 45 CFR 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), ensure that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate agrees in writing to the same restrictions, conditions and requirements that apply to Business Associate. Current Subcontractors are listed in Annex B.
- 3.4.2 Notify Covered Entity in writing **at least thirty (30) days** before a new Subcontractor first receives PHI, and update Annex B.
- 3.4.3 Send no PHI to a Subcontractor whose agreement under 3.4.1 is not in effect.

3.5 **Access.** Make PHI in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity as necessary for Covered Entity's obligations under 45 CFR 164.524. Business Associate doesn't keep copies of patient records (Covered Entity's practice-management system is the record), so a request received directly from an Individual is **forwarded to Covered Entity within five (5) Business Days**, and Business Associate helps retrieve any PHI it holds.

3.6 **Amendment.** Make any amendment to PHI in a Designated Record Set as directed or agreed to by Covered Entity under 45 CFR 164.526. Requests received directly from an Individual are forwarded to Covered Entity within five (5) Business Days.

3.7 **Accounting of disclosures.** Maintain and make available to Covered Entity the information required for an accounting of disclosures under 45 CFR 164.528, within ten (10) Business Days of Covered Entity's request.

3.8 **Covered Entity's obligations.** To the extent Business Associate carries out an obligation of Covered Entity under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164, comply with the requirements of Subpart E that apply to Covered Entity in performing it.

3.9 **Books and records.** Make its internal practices, books and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary for determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

3.10 **Location.** Store and process PHI only in the United States, including at Subcontractors.

3.11 **Access from outside the United States.** `[Open: sponsor decides. Option 1: no person accesses PHI from outside the United States. Option 2: access from outside the United States only by the named roles in Annex B, under the same safeguards.]`

## 4. Permitted uses and disclosures
4.1 **Services.** Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the Services, for the purposes and data categories listed in Annex A.

4.2 **Required by law.** Business Associate may use or disclose PHI as Required by Law.

4.3 **Minimum necessary.** Business Associate requests, uses and discloses only the minimum PHI necessary, limited to the data categories in Annex A, consistent with Covered Entity's minimum necessary policies.

4.4 **No use Covered Entity couldn't make.** Business Associate may not use or disclose PHI in a manner that would violate Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity.

4.5 **No AI training, no sale.** Business Associate will not use PHI, and will not permit any Subcontractor to use PHI, to train, fine-tune or otherwise improve any artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model, and will not sell PHI or receive remuneration in exchange for it.

4.6 **De-identified data.** Business Associate will not de-identify PHI, or use or disclose de-identified data derived from it, for any purpose without Covered Entity's prior written consent.

4.7 **Management and administration; data aggregation.** Not permitted under this Agreement. `[Counsel: confirm. If added, section 5.3 needs the HHS "Option 2" wording.]`

## 5. Term and termination
5.1 **Term.** This Agreement starts on the effective date and ends when the Service Agreement ends, or on termination under 5.2, whichever is sooner.

5.2 **Termination for cause.** Business Associate authorizes termination of this Agreement by Covered Entity if Covered Entity determines that Business Associate has violated a material term of this Agreement `[and Business Associate has not cured the violation within [30] days of written notice]`.

5.3 **Return or destruction.** Within **thirty (30) days** after termination for any reason, Business Associate returns to Covered Entity, or at Covered Entity's written choice destroys, all PHI it or its Subcontractors still maintain in any form, keeps no copies, and gives Covered Entity a **written certificate of destruction**. Where return or destruction is not feasible (for example, encrypted backups that expire on a fixed schedule of at most [30] days (30 daily backups)), Business Associate tells Covered Entity, extends the protections of this Agreement to that PHI, and limits further use and disclosure to the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, until it is destroyed. `[Counsel: confirm the backup wording.]`

5.4 **Survival.** Business Associate's obligations under 5.3 survive termination.

## 6. Obligations of Covered Entity
6.1 Covered Entity notifies Business Associate of any limitation in its Notice of Privacy Practices (45 CFR 164.520), any change in or revocation of an Individual's permission, and any restriction it has agreed to (45 CFR 164.522), to the extent it may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

6.2 Covered Entity will not ask Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner not permissible under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity.

## 7. Miscellaneous
7.1 **Regulatory references.** A reference to the HIPAA Rules means the section as in effect or as amended.

7.2 **Amendment.** The parties will amend this Agreement as needed to comply with the HIPAA Rules and other applicable law. A change to Annex A that adds a data category or purpose needs Covered Entity's written agreement before it takes effect. A change to Annex B follows 3.4.2.

7.3 **Interpretation.** Any ambiguity is interpreted to permit compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

7.4 **Order of precedence.** For PHI, this Agreement prevails over the Service Agreement.
